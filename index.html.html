Method notes: Positive/negative UDL moment coefficients (wL²/10, wL²/9) follow the ACI approximate moment coefficient method for continuous beams with roughly equal spans, UDL ≤ 3× point load. Point-load moments are exact: M+ = 2Pa²b²/L³ (midspan-type), M- = Pa²b/L² (support-type), superposed onto the UDL moment. Shear multiplier of 1.15 approximates increased shear at the face of the first interior support. The As column shown here is the direct result of As = Mu / (3.9·d) — the same simplified formula cell in your workbook (fy = 60 ksi basis, φ=0.90, j≈0.87, Hassoun & Al-Manaseer). Pass/fail checks additionally verify against As_min = ρ_min·b·d and As_max = ρ_max·b·d behind the scenes, same as the sheet. For irregular spans, unbalanced loading, or final construction documents, verify with a full continuous-beam analysis (moment distribution / matrix / ETABS).
